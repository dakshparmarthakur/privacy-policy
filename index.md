We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in the event of an error or crash in the app, data and information are collected (through third-party products) on your phone called **Log Data**.

## 4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. These are sent to your browser or app environment from the websites you visit and are stored on your device's internal memory.

This Service does not use these cookies explicitly. However, the game uses third-party code and libraries (such as the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Unity SDKs) that may use cookies or device tokens to collect information and target relevant advertisements. 

You have the option to accept or refuse these tracking identifiers via the built-in **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** consent form presented upon launching the app in applicable regions (such as the EEA/UK).
