# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 26, 2026**

**Games Of Daksh** ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by **Daksh Thakur**, develops and publishes free-to-play, ad-supported mobile games.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use our games and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features and third-party services used in the app, the following information may be collected or processed:

* **Advertising identifiers**, such as your device's Google Advertising ID.
* **Device information**, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, and other technical information.
* **Diagnostic and usage information**, such as crash reports, error information, and information about how the app performs.

Games Of Daksh does not operate its own remote database for storing your gameplay data.

---

## 2. Local Data and Device Storage

Some game information is stored locally on your device, including:

* High scores and gameplay preferences.
* Background preferences.
* A custom background image, if you choose to select one.

If you use the custom background feature, the app may request permission to access images or media on your device so that you can select an image.

The selected image is used for the game's custom background feature and is stored locally on your device. Games Of Daksh does not upload your custom background image to its own server or store it in a remote database.

You can remove your custom background using the available in-app controls.

---

## 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services and software development kits (SDKs) to provide advertising and related functionality.

These services may collect or process certain information, including advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and diagnostic information, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The third-party services currently used by the Service include:

* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [Google Advertising Technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
* [Google User Messaging Platform Information](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy)

Please review the applicable privacy policies and information pages of these providers to understand how they handle information.

---

## 4. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, the Service may present a consent message before requesting ads or processing certain information for advertising purposes.

The app may use Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to manage consent where applicable.

Depending on your location, you may be given choices regarding personalized advertising and other data processing.

Your choices may affect the type of advertisements you see. Where permitted, you may still receive non-personalized or limited advertisements.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google Uses Information From Sites and Apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

## 5. Log and Diagnostic Data

If the Service experiences an error or crash, third-party services used by the app may collect diagnostic information.

This information may include:

* Internet Protocol (IP) address.
* Device manufacturer and model.
* Operating system version.
* App configuration and technical information.
* The date and time of an error or crash.
* Crash reports and other diagnostic information.

This information may be used to identify, investigate, and resolve technical problems and improve the stability and performance of the Service.

---

## 6. How Information May Be Used

Information processed by the Service or its third-party providers may be used to:

* Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
* Display and measure advertisements.
* Process consent preferences.
* Improve app stability and performance.
* Detect, investigate, and resolve technical issues.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Games Of Daksh does not sell gameplay data stored locally on your device.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

---

## 7. Sharing of Information

Games Of Daksh does not maintain its own remote database containing your gameplay data.

However, certain information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide their services, including:

* Advertising.
* Consent management.
* Diagnostic and technical functionality.

These third-party providers handle information in accordance with their applicable privacy policies.

---

## 8. Data Retention

Gameplay settings and other locally stored game data generally remain on your device until you:

* Remove the data using an available in-app option.
* Clear the app's data.
* Uninstall the app.

Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal obligations.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Third-party services integrated into the app may process certain technical information as necessary to provide their services.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information in connection with our Service, please contact us using the information below. We will take appropriate steps based on the information available to us.

---

## 10. Data Security

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
